What is Ol Chiki AI?#

Ol Chiki AI is an open-source research initiative focused on building AI models for the Santali language and the Ol Chiki script — from character recognition to speech, translation, and linguistic analysis.

Why Santali and Ol Chiki Need Dedicated AI#

Santali has a rich and complex grammatical structure with deep morphology — the way words are formed and meaning shifts with context is fundamentally different from Indo-Aryan languages. Ol Chiki, invented in 1925 by Pandit Raghunath Murmu, was designed specifically to represent these sounds. It is an entirely original script, unrelated to Devanagari, Bengali, or Roman. Dedicated models, datasets, and research are required.

Reading and Recognising Text#

Santali documents, books, and handwritten notes exist largely on paper. AI models can scan and digitise this material, making it searchable and accessible without manual transcription. This is the first step in bringing the written Santali heritage into the digital world.

Speech#

With a foundation in text recognition, AI speech models can read Santali text aloud and recognise spoken Santali — extending access to those who rely on audio rather than text, and enabling voice-driven applications in the language.

Translation and Access#

Once Santali text and speech can be processed, AI translation opens the language to the wider world. Santali speakers can access educational content, public services, and information in their own language — and Santali literature and culture becomes reachable to others.

Language Resources#

Every model trained and every dataset collected builds a growing corpus for Santali — annotated text, speech recordings, and lexical data. These resources form the foundation that future researchers, educators, and developers can build upon.

Open Source#

All work produced by this initiative is open-source and freely available at GitHub.
